Who we are

NC Gold Buyers Group has been buying gold and silver from North Carolina families since 1989. We're based in Raleigh, and this event series is our way of bringing that same straightforward, professional evaluation to communities across the state — starting with this stop at Brier Creek.

No pressure, no pushy sales pitch — just a fair look at what you've got and a clear offer, from a business your neighbors have trusted for over three decades.
